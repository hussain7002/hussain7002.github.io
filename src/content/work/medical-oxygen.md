## The problem

COVID-19's second wave exposed how fragile hospital oxygen supply was. States then installed a lot of new infrastructure quickly and needed technical help to run and maintain it safely.

## My role

Under USAID- and Gates Foundation-funded initiatives, I was technical adviser on medical oxygen to the governments of Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu and Telangana, covering about 100 district hospitals.

## Approach

- **Advice across the whole oxygen system:** PSA plants, liquid oxygen tanks and transport, medical gas pipelines, oxygen concentrators and how they are administered, and fire safety.
- **Facility assessments,** which I carried out myself, to see what each hospital had and needed.
- **A demand estimation tool** that works out a facility's oxygen needs from bed capacity and other parameters, for planning.
- **Regional guidance.** I contributed to WHO SEARO's regional technical guidance and training on oxygen and respiratory care equipment, working with WHO country teams across the region, including Indonesia and Timor-Leste.

## Outcome

WHO SEARO has published the regional guidance:

- [Regional manual with operating guides on respiratory care equipment and devices](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789292800055)
- [Generic training modules on oxygen/respiratory care equipment](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789292800062)
