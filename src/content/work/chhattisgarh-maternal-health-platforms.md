## The problem

SUMAN is India's programme setting the standard for how maternal health services should be delivered. It assesses facilities with a long set of checklists that were hard to administer on paper and harder still to aggregate across a state. Maternal health data was also split across government portals, so no single view was available to officials.

## My role

I owned both products end to end: functional design, the vendor build, user acceptance testing, the security audit, project management and business analysis.

## What I built

- **e-SUMAN**, an app that administers the SUMAN checklists digitally, adds citizen feedback, and aggregates results at state level for facility-level resource allocation. It was deployed across about 300 health facilities in more than 30 districts. I also helped the state team analyse the data it produced.
- **A maternal health dashboard** that brings data from two government portals into one view for state officials, with harmonised indicators.

## Outcome

Both products were delivered to the state health department.
