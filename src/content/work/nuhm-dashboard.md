## The problem

The National Urban Health Mission (NUHM) runs primary health care for more than 500 million people in India's cities. Its data lives in many national portals, each built for its own programme. To answer basic questions about facilities, staff, drugs, diagnostics, teleconsultation and spend, officers had to read those portals one at a time.

## My role

I lead the product design of the mission's national dashboard. That covers requirement analysis with senior officers and programme staff, specifications, indicator logic and user journeys, and a working prototype.

## Approach

- **Two audiences, two views.** Officers and executives need decisions at a glance. Office staff and the Project Management Unit need the finer detail of running the programme. The dashboard serves both without forcing one view on the other.
- **One reporting layer.** I designed the reporting architecture: dimensional data models and a standard catalogue of KPIs and indicators that bring several national health information systems into a single enterprise reporting layer.
- **A prototype, not a document.** I built a working prototype with AI-assisted development, so officers could react to something real rather than a specification.

## Outcome

The prototype is now the base that the implementing agency builds the live system on.
